## 2. OPEN: the decode "sluggishness" investigation

Long thread; the diagnosis **changed twice**, so read the conclusion, not the
journey. Full detail in memory `vitrine-decode-openpos-investigation`.

### The corrected conclusion (trust this)
**It is NOT an open-position bug.** The grid opens at the **top** (`vadj=0`,
proven by a `VDBG-GRIDHOME` probe). A `scroll_to(0)`-on-populate fix was written
and **reverted** — it was solving a phantom.

**Actual root cause — over-bind poisons the decode centre.** With default 240px
icons (`DEFAULT_ICON=2`, window.rs:130) in a 1100×720 window only ~10 cells are
truly visible, but on initial realize `GtkGridView` **binds ~225 cells (pos
0–224)**. `flush_pending` (window.rs:~692) sets `visible_center = (lo+hi)/2` of
bound cells = **112**, and it sticks. `pop_best_load` (window.rs:~744) then
decodes nearest-to-112 first, so the ~10 actually-visible **top** cells (pos
0–11) are ~100 away and decode **near-last** — the top of the screen sits blank
while the decoder grinds off-screen cells. That is the months-old "sluggish /
feels like it opened in the middle." It's the center-out-vs-reading-order
perception issue, with a concrete trigger.

### Evidence (A/B, hands-off, cold)
- Controlled Name-sort vs `script:Largest first`: both open at **exactly c112**
  → sort-independent (kills the "script reorder" theory), and hands-off (kills
  "user scrolled").
- Log stats per run: 225 `visible=true`, 64 prefetch, fills uniform pos 0–288,
  top cells (0–14) *do* decode, `center` never leaves 112.

### Proposed fix — NOT chosen, NOT implemented (awaiting user)
- **(a)** derive `visible_center` from the **scroll offset** (vadjustment), not
  bound-cell positions — immune to over-binding; principled.
- **(b)** bias `pop_best_load` toward the **top of the visible range** (lowest
  position) instead of geometric centre — one-liner, matches "viewport = top
  priority."
- **Separate perf question:** why does GtkGridView over-bind ~225 cells for a
  ~10-cell viewport? Worth its own look.

### Verify any fix with the harness
`scratchpad/ab-openpos.sh` (in the session scratchpad) backs up settings.ini,
runs two hands-off cold opens (Name vs script sort), prints opening `GRIDFILL`
centres, restores settings. After a real fix, expect the centre to drop from
~112 toward the true top (~5–10). Requires a flatpak rebuild first:
`flatpak-builder --user --install --force-clean build-dir build-aux/io.github.superuser_miguel.Vitrine.yml`
